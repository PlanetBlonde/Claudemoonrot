Shipping & returns

Shipping

We ship within the United States only. Shipping is a flat $6 per order, no matter how many stones are in it.

Orders ship within 3 business days, wrapped carefully and packed so nothing rattles. You'll get an email receipt from Stripe when you order.

Arrived broken?

Email a photo to moon@foxandmoonrot.com within 7 days of delivery. We'll replace it or refund you in full, your choice.

Changed your mind?

Return it unused within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. You cover return shipping. Email us first so we know it's coming.

What you're buying

Everything here is sold as decor and for enjoyment. Natural stones vary in color and pattern; "one of a kind" means the exact stone pictured, and "intuitively chosen" means we pick yours from the batch shown. Nothing sold here diagnoses, treats or cures anything.

Payments

Checkout is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
