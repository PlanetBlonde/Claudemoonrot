Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026. Fox & Moonrot Mercantile is a small family shop in Massachusetts, and this page explains, in plain English, what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how we keep it safe. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • When you buy something: your name, email address, shipping address, and the items you ordered. Your card details go straight to Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number.
  • When you join the launch letter: your email address and the date you signed up.
  • When you email us: whatever you choose to put in the email.
  • When you visit the site: our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time) for security and troubleshooting. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.
  • In your own browser: the site remembers your day/night setting and what is in your shopping bag using your browser's local storage. That information stays on your device and is never sent to us until you check out.

How we use it

  • To process payment, pack, and ship your order.
  • To send your receipt and answer questions about your order.
  • To email the launch letter and occasional shop news, only if you signed up. Every letter has an easy way out; reply "unsubscribe" or email us.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site running.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not use it for advertising.

Who else sees it, and how

We share only what each service needs to do its job, through that service's secure (encrypted) connection:

  • Stripe processes payments and collects your name, email, card, and shipping address at checkout. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Hostinger hosts this website and our email, so order notices, signups, and emails you send us are stored on their servers.
  • The shipping carrier (such as USPS) receives your name and shipping address on the label.
  • Our merch partner, Fourthwall, handles hoodie orders at shop.foxandmoonrot.com under its own privacy policy. Those orders do not pass through this site.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser contacts Google and shares your IP address when pages load.
  • The law: we will disclose information if required by law, such as a valid court order or for tax records.

How we protect it

  • The whole site runs over HTTPS, so information is encrypted between your browser and us.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Stripe, which is certified to the PCI DSS Level 1 security standard.
  • Signup lists and order records are stored in a private folder on our host that cannot be reached from the web.
  • Only the two of us who run the shop can access orders and email, using password-protected accounts.
  • We keep only what we need. Signup lists are deleted when you unsubscribe; order records are kept as long as tax law requires (generally up to 7 years) and then deleted.

No system is perfectly secure, but if we ever learn your information was exposed, we will tell you promptly.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, correct it, or delete it (except records we must keep for taxes). Email moon@foxandmoonrot.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

This shop is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update it here and change the date at the top.
